Commercial Projects Delivered With Confidence

DMWright Solutions LLC specializes in commercial construction projects, including renovations, new builds, and tenant improvements. Our expertise spans apartment complexes, church renovations, mixed-use developments, and new construction for commercial salons.

We understand that commercial projects run on schedules, budgets and codes. Our safety-first project management keeps your site organized and compliant, your stakeholders informed, and your business moving forward.

Commercial

Commercial Services

Renovations, new builds and tenant improvements across four core specialties.

Apartment Complexes

Renovations and upgrades that improve resident experience and protect property value.

  • Interior & exterior renovations
  • Unit remodels and upgrades
  • Common area improvements (lobbies, hallways, amenities)
  • Structural and foundation work
  • Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC upgrades
  • Code compliance and ADA accessibility updates

Church Renovations

Respectful, high-quality work for worship spaces — from sanctuaries to parking lots.

  • Sanctuary and fellowship hall remodeling
  • Custom carpentry, pew refinishing, and stage construction
  • Lighting and acoustics enhancement
  • Roof and structural repairs
  • Historic preservation and modernization
  • Parking lot and landscape improvements

Mixed-Use Commercial Renovations

Flexible build-outs for retail, office and restaurant tenants that keep projects on schedule.

  • Retail and office space conversions
  • Multi-functional layout redesigns
  • Restaurant and storefront build-outs
  • Tenant improvement projects
  • Facade enhancements and exterior upgrades
  • Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) system installations

New Construction — Commercial Salons

Ground-up salon construction built to state board standards and finished to a high-end level.

  • Ground-up salon construction
  • Custom cabinetry, stations, and interior build-outs*
  • High-end finish installations (mirrors, lighting, flooring)
  • Specialized plumbing for shampoo stations
  • HVAC and ventilation systems for salon environments
  • Compliance with state board regulations and industry standards

*Custom cabinetry, stations and interior build-outs vary depending on the architect used.
